# Mutual NDA (Common Paper MNDA v1.0) — Issues for PT Sinar Data Nusantara

Reviewed for **PT Sinar Data Nusantara** as **Receiving Party of Northwind’s API docs/pricing** and **Disclosing Party of sample shipment data containing customer names, phone numbers, and delivery addresses**. Parties are fictional; paper is Common Paper Mutual NDA Standard Terms v1.0 (CC BY 4.0) with a filled Cover Page.

Quotes are taken from the attached NDA (`NDA-Northwind-SinarData.docx`). The NDA does **not** invent any clause that is not in the paper; where PT Sinar Data needs something the paper does not say, that is marked **SILENT**.

| 1 | Cover Page — Purpose: “Evaluating a pilot integration of Northwind’s analytics API into PT Sinar Data Nusantara’s logistics app, including access to sample shipment data.” | The Purpose allows unlimited use of that sample data for the pilot. Sample shipment data will include **customer names, phone numbers, and delivery addresses** (personal data). Nothing stops Northwind from retaining, analyzing beyond the pilot, or using the data to train or improve Northwind models/products. | **High** | Add after that sentence: “Sample shipment data shall be limited to the minimum fields reasonably necessary for the Purpose, shall not include more customer personal data than needed for the pilot, and shall not be used by Northwind to train, improve, or benchmark any model, product, or service other than the specific pilot integration.” | If Northwind refuses a full no-train clause, accept “shall not use Personal Data to train or improve any Northwind product or model” and a hard cap on fields (names + city-level address only, no full address/phone if the pilot can run without them). |
| 2 | Cover Page — Term of Confidentiality: “☒ 1 year from Effective Date, but in the case of trade secrets until Confidential Information is no longer considered a trade secret under applicable laws.” | The MNDA also expires after 1 year (Cover Page — MNDA Term). Confidentiality dies one year after signing even if the pilot becomes a production integration, and even though the shipment data is customer personal data, not a trade secret. After year 1, Section 3 exceptions can be argued to strip residual protection. | **High** | Replace the checked Term of Confidentiality with: “☒ 3 years from Effective Date, but in the case of trade secrets until Confidential Information is no longer considered a trade secret under applicable laws.” | 2 years from Effective Date, **or** 3 years measured from each disclosure (whichever is later). Do not accept 1 year if customer personal data will be sent. |
| 3 | Cover Page — Governing Law: “Governing Law: Delaware” | Sinar is an Indonesian private company. Law No. 24/2009 Art. 31(1) requires Indonesian in agreements involving an Indonesian private entity; Art. 31(2) requires a second language of the foreign party and/or English. The paper is English-only. Choice of Delaware law is not itself a crime, but an all-English paper is off-side of the language rule (practice: usually not void — SEMA 3/2023 — but it is a real compliance and interpretive risk, and the Constitutional Court in 2025 did **not** rewrite Art. 31). | **High** | Replace with: “Governing Law: the laws of the Republic of Indonesia. This MNDA shall be made in the Indonesian language as the principal version, with an English version for Northwind Analytics, Inc.; in the event of any conflict, the Indonesian version shall control.” | Bilingual Indonesian + English, Indonesian controlling. If Northwind insists on Delaware law, keep bilingual Indonesian/English and **require exclusive Indonesian courts** (see #4). Do not accept English-only. |
| 4 | Cover Page — Jurisdiction: “Jurisdiction: courts located in New Castle, DE” **and** Standard Terms § 9: “Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of such Jurisdiction in any such suit, action, or proceeding.” | Exclusive Delaware courts mean Sinar must sue in the US to protect its own customer data. A Delaware judgment is **not directly enforceable** in Indonesia: no Indonesia–US Hague 1971/2019 judgment convention; Art. 436(1) Rv generally bars direct enforcement; CMS and Indonesian practice: re-litigate as a fresh claim, with public-order discretion. Injunctions under § 10 are likewise hard to execute here. | **High** | Cover Page: “Jurisdiction: the courts of the Republic of Indonesia.” Plus add at the end of § 9: “Notwithstanding the Cover Page, the parties agree that the courts of the Republic of Indonesia shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute relating to this MNDA.” | Split forum: Indonesian courts for any dispute involving Sinar’s customer personal data or Indonesian-law obligations; Delaware courts only for other disputes. Avoid exclusive Delaware courts for everything. |
| 5 | **SILENT** — MNDA Modifications: “None.” The Standard Terms never define “personal data,” never mention Law No. 27 of 2022, never mention a personal-data incident, and never require processor terms. | Sinar is about to disclose **personal data** of its customers. Common Paper MNDA v1.0 is a pure trade-secret/NDA. PDP Law Art. 2 applies extraterritorially; Art. 56(2) requires the recipient country to have equal or higher protection, or a binding adequate instrument, or subject consent; Art. 46 requires 3×24 hour breach notice to the subject and the authority. The NDA says none of this. | **High** | Replace “None.” with “As follows:” and insert: (1) **Personal Data** — process only for the Purpose, comply with Law 27/2022 and implementing rules, return/destroy in 10 Business Days, notify the other party within 1×24 hours of a Personal Data Protection Failure affecting its Confidential Information; (2) **Service Providers** — written processor terms (Purpose-only, no-less-protective security/confidentiality, no onward disclosure without written approval, deletion at end); (3) **No Modification of Law** — nothing reduces mandatory law, including the PDP Law. | If Northwind will not add three cover-page mods, accept a short annex: PDP Law applies; no training on Sinar data; processor flow-down; 1×24 / 3×24 breach notice. Do not send live customer names/phones/addresses without some written personal-data terms. |
| 6 | Standard Terms § 2(b): “except that the Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees, agents, advisors, contractors and other representatives having a reasonable need to know for the Purpose, provided these representatives are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective of the Disclosing Party than the applicable terms in this MNDA and the Receiving Party remains responsible for their compliance with this MNDA” | Northwind can give Sinar’s customer data to any contractor, cloud host, or “agent” on a simple NDA-level confidentiality clause. PDP Law and PP 33/2026 expect controller–processor terms (PP 33/2026: 11 minimum processor clauses). “Confidentiality no less protective than this MNDA” is not a processor agreement. | **Medium** | Add to § 2: “Confidential Information that is or includes Personal Data shall be processed only for the Purpose and in accordance with Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection and its implementing regulations.” Plus the Service Providers cover-page modification in #5. | Keep § 2(b) as is for ordinary contractors, but require written processor terms **before** any personal data is shared, and a list of subprocessors. |
| 7 | Standard Terms § 3(a)–(d): obligations “do not apply to information that it can demonstrate: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (b) it rightfully knew or possessed prior to receipt from the Disclosing Party without confidentiality restrictions; (c) it rightfully obtained from a third party without confidentiality restrictions; or (d) it independently developed without using or referencing the Confidential Information.” | If customer data leaks from Northwind and is then posted online, § 3(a) lets Northwind argue it is “publicly available.” If Northwind later obtains similar logistics data from another customer, § 3(c)–(d) can be used to say Sinar’s customer list is not protected. Personal data should not fall out of the NDA this way. | **Medium** | Add to § 3: “Subsection (a) does not apply to Personal Data that becomes publicly available as a result of a Personal Data Protection Failure of the Receiving Party. ## What the NDA is silent on (must be added, not assumed)

The NDA does **not** contain, and PT Sinar Data should not treat as agreed:

- any definition of **personal data** or any reference to **Law No. 27 of 2022**;
- a **processor / subprocessor** flow-down for customer data sent to Northwind’s cloud or engineers;
- a **no-training / no-product-improvement** rule;
- a **personal-data incident notice** (PDP Law Art. 46 is 3×24 hours to the subject and the authority — the NDA has no party-to-party notice);
- **Indonesian-language** text (Law No. 24/2009 Art. 31);
- **Indonesian courts** or any recognition that a Delaware judgment cannot be executed in Indonesia;
- a **returns deadline** tighter than “promptly after written request” plus unlimited backups;
- a **security schedule**.

## Order to concede (if Northwind only gives a little)

Give these last, not first:

1. Forum #4 and personal-data mods #5 — do not sign without them if live customer names/phones/addresses will be sent.
2. Governing language #3 and confidentiality term #2.
3. § 2 PDP sentence, § 3 personal-data carve-out, § 6 deletion clock.
4. § 4 recording, § 8 personal-data carve-out from AS IS, § 11 notices.
5. § 5 termination, § 2(c) security schedule, § 11 assignment.

Market for a small-company MNDA is more generous on #8–#16 than on #1–#5. #1–#5 are the deal.
