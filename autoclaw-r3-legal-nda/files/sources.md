## A. Laws and rules actually relied on

### 1. Law No. 24 of 2009 on the Flag, Language, and Emblem of the State, and the National Anthem (Indonesia) — **VERIFIED CURRENT**

**Why we use it:** Cover Page is English-only. Article 31 requires Indonesian in agreements involving an Indonesian private entity, and a second language of the foreign party and/or English when a foreign party is involved.

- **Official listing (status “Berlaku” / in force):** [peraturan.bpk.go.id — UU No. 24 Tahun 2009](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/38661/uu-no-24-tahun-2009)
- **Official PDF:** [UU Nomor 24 Tahun 2009.pdf](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/27970/UU%20Nomor%2024%20Tahun%2009.pdf) — search result of this PDF shows Pasal 31 and the Elucidation stating that “perjanjian” includes international agreements. The BPK viewer is bot-gated; article text below was cross-checked on a public statute mirror.

**Article 31(1)** (used): Indonesian **must** be used in memoranda of understanding or agreements involving state institutions, the Government of the Republic of Indonesia, Indonesian private institutions, or individual Indonesian citizens.

**Article 31(2)** (used): an MoU or agreement under (1) that involves a foreign party must also be written in the national language of that foreign party **and/or English**.

**Elucidation of Article 31(1):** “agreements” includes international agreements.

- Mirror used for full Pasal 31 text: [pasal.id — Pasal 31 UU 24/2009](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-24-tahun-2009/pasal-31)

**Current?** Yes. No repeal of Article 31. The Constitutional Court was asked to rewrite “wajib digunakan” in 2025 and **refused the petition** (see item 7). SEMA 3/2023 (Supreme Court circular) treats missing Indonesian as generally **not** making the contract void — so this is a real compliance point, not a signature-killing point (see item 8).

### 3. Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (Indonesia) — **VERIFIED CURRENT**

**Why we use it:** PT Sinar Data will disclose sample shipment data containing **customer names, phone numbers, and delivery addresses** (personal data) to a Delaware company. The NDA never mentions personal data.

- **Official listing (status “Berlaku”):** [peraturan.bpk.go.id — UU No. 27 Tahun 2022](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-tahun-2022)

| Article | What it says (used in the review) | Verified |
|---|---|---|

| **Pasal 16(2)** | Processing principles, including (g): personal data is **destroyed and/or deleted after the retention period ends** or on the data subject’s request, unless another law says otherwise. | Yes — Komdigi JDIH full text of Pasal 16 |

### 5. Can a Delaware court judgment be enforced in Indonesia? — **NO, not directly. Hague route closed. Domestic statute article numbers partly UNVERIFIED.**

This is one of the three questions the instruction required.

#### 5.1 Hague Conventions (official HCCH status tables) — **VERIFIED**

There is **no** Hague convention in force between Indonesia and the United States that would make a Delaware commercial judgment directly enforceable in Indonesia.

| Instrument | Indonesia | United States | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1971 Convention** on Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Judgments in Civil and Commercial Matters (HCCH cid=78) | **Not a party** | **Not a party** | [HCCH status table cid=78](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=78) — parties: Albania, Cyprus, Kuwait, Netherlands, Portugal only |
| **2019 Convention** on Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Judgments in Civil or Commercial Matters (HCCH cid=137) | **Not a party** | **Signed 2 March 2022, not ratified** (no EIF for the US) | [HCCH status table cid=137](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=137) |
| **1973 Maintenance Obligations Convention** (HCCH cid=85) — not relevant to an MNDA, listed only to close the “is there any judgment convention?” question | **Not a party** | **Not a party** | [HCCH status table cid=85](https://www.hcch.net/en/instruments/conventions/status-table/?cid=85) |

#### 5.2 Indonesian domestic rule — conclusion **VERIFIED** (secondary); **Rv 436 / HIR 227a article text UNVERIFIED against an official statute PDF**

**Conclusion (what we tell the CEO):** a Delaware judgment is **not directly executable** in Indonesia. The creditor ordinarily files a **fresh claim** in an Indonesian court and uses the foreign judgment as evidence. The Indonesian judge has discretion to refuse on public order / public policy.

- Primary secondary source I read in full: [CMS Expert Guide — Indonesia](https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/cms-expert-guide-to-recognition-and-enforcement-of-judgements/indonesia) — “In general, foreign judgments are **not enforceable** in Indonesia (see Article 436(1) of the *Reglement op de Rechtvordering* (Rv)).” Enforcement only via treaty, a specific national law (e.g. Commercial Code Art. 724 on sea transport), or a **fresh claim**. No exequatur. Months to years.
- Corroborating: [globallawexperts.com 2026 guide](https://globallawexperts.com/news/foreign-judgment-enforcement-indonesia) (fresh domestic suit; Hague Convention does not help).

**UNVERIFIED items (do not treat as primary law):**

- The **exact official text of Rv Article 436(1)** — I did not retrieve a government PDF of *Herzien Inlandsch Reglement* / *Reglement op de Rechtvordering*. I rely on CMS for the article number.
- **HIR Article 227a** (the other article often cited for execution of foreign judgments after a *Percobaan* / re-trial). I did not retrieve official HIR text in this session. **UNVERIFIED.**
- The *Hirschmann Kleden Percobaan* line of cases: I did not pull a primary judgment PDF in this session. **UNVERIFIED** as a case citation. The **fresh-claim** conclusion does not depend on that case name.
- No Indonesia–US **bilateral** enforcement treaty was found. Absence of a treaty is consistent with HCCH (above) and CMS; I did not run a full treaty-index search beyond HCCH.

**Practical point used in the redline:** exclusive Delaware courts (Cover Page + Standard Terms § 9) therefore give PT Sinar Data a judgment it may not be able to collect in Indonesia. That is why we ask for Indonesian courts.

### 7. Constitutional Court Decision No. 127/PUU-XXIII/2025 (Indonesia) — **VERIFIED** (official MK synopsis)

**Why we use it:** to confirm that Article 31 UU 24/2009 was **not** rewritten in 2025, so we still cite Article 31 as it stands.

- **Official ikhtisar (synopsis) PDF I read in full:** [s.mkri.id — Ikhtisar Putusan 127/PUU-XXIII/2025](https://s.mkri.id/public/content/persidangan/sinopsis/ikhtisar_4678_2705_ikhtisar%20Put%20127_2025%20(ACC%20PM%20III).pdf)
- **Official press release (28 August 2025):** [mkri.id press PDF](https://www.mkri.id/public/content/infoumum/press/pdf/press_1756373777_54fff4b6bb2f605eeb0f.pdf)

**Holding:** petitioners asked the Court to read “wajib digunakan” as making foreign-language contracts **void**, and to require simultaneous bilingual execution. The Court **did not accept the petition** (*tidak dapat diterima*) on 28 August 2025, finding it obscure (*obsuur*). **Article 31 remains as enacted.** I do **not** cite this decision as changing the law.

A longer judgment PDF (`putusan_mkri_13097_1756365807.pdf`) was only partially readable (first pages / petition identity). The synopsis is the source for the *amar*.
