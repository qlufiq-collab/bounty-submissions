# Cash handout card (poidh bounty base/1510)

Business-card sized handout (3.5 x 2 in, 300 dpi, pure black and white) explaining why paying with cash matters for privacy and freedom.

- `card_front.png`: front, print-ready, 1050 x 600 px
- `card_back.png`: back, print-ready, 1050 x 600 px
- `card_sheet.png`: both sides on one sheet, for preview

Front: "CASH IS FREEDOM." / "Private. Offline. Yours." / "Thank you for taking cash. Read the back."

Back: why I pay in cash (private, works offline, for everyone, can't be switched off), then the call to action: "Use it or lose it. Pay cash when you can. Keep accepting it. Pass this card on."

The illustrations were generated with Google Flow in a banknote-engraving style and converted to pure black and white. Typography is Inter and DejaVu Serif. Released under CC0: print and share freely.
