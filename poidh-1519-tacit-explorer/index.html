How to read this page

Bitcoin side. Tacit assets live as envelopes hidden in Taproot witnesses. Anyone can decode them; amounts are inside Pedersen commitments.
The bridge. A permissionless Bitcoin header relay on Ethereum, plus ZK proofs of what Bitcoin contains (“reflection”), lets value cross in both directions.
Ethereum / Base side. The immutable ConfidentialPool keeps notes as hashes in a Merkle tree; a separate ETH-only “EVM pool” runs on Ethereum and Base.
Where numbers come from. Each card names its source: chain read from a public RPC or mempool.space in your browser, or indexer Tacit’s public API. The cross-checks compare the two.
