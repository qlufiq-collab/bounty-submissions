## Bai — Rent for Her Own Room

**In one breath:** From the age of twelve Bai paid her aunt rent for her own childhood bedroom, because a gift can be taken back and a room you pay for is yours, and the only creed that has ever made sense to her is the one preached by the people who killed her parents.

Her mother and father died in a lab explosion when she was small, and everyone in Pafogai Du knows who did it. Her aunt gave her a room and two meals a day, and nothing else. At twelve Bai started earning her own money, writing up other children's Minpentai games in neat notebooks and selling them to their parents, and the first thing she did with it was put an envelope on the kitchen table: rent. Her aunt took it without a word. Bai was glad. A gift could be taken back. Rent made the room hers.

That was the arithmetic she lived by: nobody owes you love, you earn it the way you earn a room. It made her the second-best player in Pafogai Du, and a cruel one. She took Zei's prize money, the money he wanted for his mother, and spent it on the islands of Redshire, because winning was the only warmth she knew. When Zei finally beat her she told a stranger that all she had was her achievements, and she had not planned to say it.

So the Arctics killed her parents, and their sermon, love as a reward for winning, is the exact story of her childhood; she can say out loud that she "kind of" agrees. Yet when Gladias came to her broken, she hugged him without asking a single question, love for weakness, the thing that sermon calls poison, and she admits she would not have done it a year ago. Now she is thinking of spending a year in Thaldur, among the Arctics, to learn how they think. She will pay her own rent there too. She still has every envelope.
