## Seila — The Open Window

**In one breath:** At fifteen Seila climbed out of her bedroom window and ran all the way to Freetown, where a boy named Jahn fed her for a week; years later she shut a door on her own daughter, and the window opened the other way.

Seila grew up in a careful Meldan house where every risk was weighed before anyone took it. At fifteen she read one post too many about the United Cities, packed a bag, and climbed out of her bedroom window. She reached Freetown with no money left on the second day, and a cook's son named Jahn fed her at the back of his mother's stall for a week, while she learned that a city can be loud, crowded and still work. When her parents came for her, she promised Jahn she would show him Veridia one day.

She went home and became the most careful person in any room. She scores suspects point by point, saw through Delwart's Silverchat mask within a day, and believes privacy is the reason Veridia is still standing. She writes about Freetown's economy, and Jahn still sends her the gossip. Gladias thinks that is all he is.

So the girl who climbed out of a window to see what the world was really like became the mother who, when her daughter said the Arctics had shown her something true, screamed at her for ten minutes and shut the door. When Seila opened it again, the window was open and Lily was gone. She knew exactly what an open window means. Now the family is back together, she says they all made mistakes that year, and she is finally keeping her promise: Jahn is coming to see Veridia.
